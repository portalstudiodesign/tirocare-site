Safety & privacy

Built with the guardrails a health app needs.

In a health app, what the assistant must not do matters as much as what it does. Every question passes through checks that run before and around the AI.

  1. You ask a question

    About diet, treatment, lab tests or how you feel.

  2. Emergency check first

    Red-flag symptoms are caught before any AI is called, and you’re pointed to urgent care.

  3. Dose guard

    Questions about changing a dose get a fixed warning and a referral to your endocrinologist.

  4. A careful answer

    Sent without your name. If the AI service is down, the built-in assistant answers instead.

  5. You stay in control

    Flag any answer from inside the app. Turn the AI off whenever you like.

No account, ever

No sign-up, no password, no profile on a server. Open the app and start.

Your data lives on your phone

Your journal, lab results and notes are stored on your device, and you can export everything at any time.

Private document reading

PDF reports are read on your phone, by an engine the app hosts itself. No third party sees the document.

AI only with your consent

Questions and photos go to Google Gemini only after you opt in, through TiroCare’s own servers. The API key never touches your phone.

Values you approve

Numbers read from a document are checked for plausibility and shown to you for confirmation before they’re saved.

Information, not diagnosis

TiroCare explains your values. It doesn’t diagnose, recommend doses or decide when you retest. That stays with your doctor.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

Is the app available in English?

The app itself is in Romanian. It’s written for patients in Romania, with the reference ranges used by Romanian labs and the thyroid medications sold there.

Is TiroCare a medical device?

No. TiroCare provides educational information and tracking tools. It doesn’t diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it doesn’t replace your endocrinologist. For any decision about diagnosis or treatment, talk to your doctor.

Will it tell me what dose to take, or when to retest?

No, on purpose. TiroCare shows where your values sit against reference ranges and explains what the tests measure. Dose changes and retest dates are decided by your doctor. You enter the date they set, and TiroCare reminds you when it gets close.

Where is my data stored?

Your journal, lab results and notes are stored on your phone, and there’s no account. If you turn on the optional AI assistant, your questions and any photos you scan are sent to Google Gemini through TiroCare’s servers. Your name is never sent. You can switch the AI off at any time and export all your data whenever you want. Details are in the privacy policy (in Romanian).

Which phones does it work on?

The web app runs in any modern browser on Android or iPhone. You can add it to your home screen and it keeps working offline. A native Android app is being prepared for Google Play.

How accurate is reading lab reports?

PDF reports issued by labs are read reliably. Photos, and especially handwriting, depend on the image, so every value is checked for plausibility and shown to you to confirm or correct before it’s saved.
